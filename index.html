Questions we get a lot

How much does a stamp cost?

Price depends on the model size and how many lines of text are on the die, so there is no single figure. Send us your wording and the model you want and we will quote it.

Which model should an engineer or architect get?

PTR and licence details usually need five or more lines. Trodat 4913 fits five lines; 4915 handles five to eight, and 4926 takes up to twelve for full PTR blocks with an office address.

Can I change the ink colour?

Yes on non-HB models — black, red, blue and green are the usual choices, subject to stock. HB models come in their fixed ink.

Can you put a logo or signature on it?

Yes. Send the clearest file you have, ideally black on white with no grey shading. Fine detail needs a larger die, so a round 40 mm or above works best for logos.

Do the daters need resetting?

The date band is adjustable by hand and covers several years. Your fixed wording around it stays on the die.

What if I find a typo after approving?

The die is already cut at that point, so a correction means a new die. Read the proof twice — especially licence and PTR numbers.
